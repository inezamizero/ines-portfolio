SWEPM

Siyoni · live

When I moved to the US, I couldn't find Catholic prayers in Kinyarwanda anywhere online; the big prayer apps are in English or French. Siyoni is a prayer website for Rwandans in the diaspora, so they can pray in their mother tongue wherever they are.

I took it from idea to launch: I wrote the project brief and design system before any code, set a zero-cost constraint (free hosting, public data sources, no database), and shipped the core first: the Rosary, three novenas, morning and evening prayers, and a daily Mass page that updates itself. Many prayer texts only exist in printed books, so I set a content rule early: traditional prayers go in freely, and copyrighted hymnal material waits for the publisher's written permission.

I also cut scope when it mattered. The song library was built, but I pulled it from the live site until it has enough songs to be useful, rather than launch an empty page.
